How the value is priced

We look at the 128 protocols in the snapshot that already trade, and for each one divide its market cap by its TVL. The middle value inside each category becomes that category's price — Lending pays about 13 cents per dollar of TVL, Dexs about 34 cents, bridges about 56 cents. Big protocols get a smaller price per dollar (the size fit says value grows slower than TVL), a fast-growing week adds a little, a long wait with no token adds a little, and running on more chains adds a little. The formula is:

implied value = TVL × category price × size factor × growth factor × age factor × chain factor

Fit: log-log slope 0.52, R² 0.19, global middle price 0.20.
